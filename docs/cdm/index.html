This model describes business concepts and the rules that connect them. It is not a database schema.

01Purpose

Namaa lets three kinds of party work on the same land record.

  • Government runs the national view and decides only public land.
  • A proprietor decides their own parcels: keep them private, offer them for sale, or open them for exploitation.
  • An investor sees only what is offered, then asks to buy or to exploit.

04Land parcel and offer

A parcel is one registered unit inside the Kingdom. It has one owner, a region, a location inland, area in hectares, power in megawatts, a crop, a cultivation status, and one current offer.

AttributeMeaning
Parcel idSuch as U-012
OwnerA proprietor party, or public land
RegionRiyadh, Jeddah, Madinah, Eastern Province, Tabuk, AlUla, Najran, Qassim, Jazan, Al Jawf
Cultivation statusActive cultivation, prepared, or under preparation
OfferThe current commercial position: private, for sale, or open for exploitation

National targets sit above the parcel: a 10,000 ha reclamation target, a planned unit count, and reclaimed area. Those figures describe the program. They are not attributes of one parcel.

OfferArabicWho may set itWhat an investor sees
privateأبقِها خاصةThe ownerNothing. The parcel is hidden.
saleبيعThe ownerThe parcel, with Buy the land
exploitمفتوح للاستغلالThe ownerThe parcel, with Exploit the land
Owner. For a private estate, the owner is the proprietor (مجموعة النخيل or الواحة العقارية). For public land, the owner is the government. The government cannot change a private offer. A proprietor cannot change another estate.

05Investment request

A request is one investor asking for one parcel. Kind is buy or exploit. Status is pending, accepted, or refused.

RuleDetail
BuyAllowed only while the offer is for sale
ExploitAllowed only while the offer is open for exploitation
One live requestOne investor, one parcel. A refused request can be sent again
WithdrawOnly while pending. Withdraw removes the request
DecisionAccept and refuse are stored on the same request. Proprietor and investor both see the result
LandWho accepts or refuses
Private parcelThat parcel’s proprietor only
Public landGovernment only

The government can see that private land is offered. It does not accept or refuse those requests. Changing the offer does not delete an existing request. The investor then sees that the parcel is no longer offered for that kind of deal.

07Operations

These concepts support the government dashboard. They do not decide a sale or an exploitation. A sensor reading does not change who owns a parcel or whether it is for sale.

ConceptWhat it records
SensorA field device: id, location, type, and status online, degraded, or offline
Irrigation zoneA group of units, duty cycle, water saved, and next run
Cultivation moduleA controlled-environment crop with climate and yield
Energy readingGeneration, pumping load, and battery across the day
Cost linePlanned and actual capital or operating cost
Program impactReclaimed hectares, water saved, and avoided emissions for the program as a whole
